About

I teach computer engineering and I am working toward a Ph.D. in machine learning. My research looks at how to train models across many devices without moving the data, using split federated learning. I am also interested in making AI agents safe and reliable for people who build them without a programming background.

I have taught for over a decade, from polytechnic diploma courses to engineering degree programmes. Alongside teaching, I have guided student projects in AI, education technology and embedded systems.
